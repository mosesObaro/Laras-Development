## Month-by-Month Detailed Roadmap

### MONTH 1 — Foundation & Study Systems
* **Primary Focus**: Goal setting, time management, Pomodoro & active recall habits, digital literacy, financial basics, career exploration, university expectations.
* **Academic Goal**: Complete Khan Academy High School Biology Review (Cell Structure & Transport); learn Anki flashcard creation.
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Establish a daily record book for measurements, fabric types, and time taken per garment.
* **Digital & AI Goal**: Organize Google Drive study folders, master Google Docs formatting, set up 2FA security on personal accounts.
* **Volunteering Goal**: Register with Nigerian Red Cross Society (Edo State Branch) or Girls' Power Initiative (Benin City).
* **Reading Assignment**: *Atomic Habits* by James Clear (Habit stacking & environment design).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. Personal 18-Month Development Plan & Weekly Timetable.
  2. Baseline monthly budget.
  3. Career-interest comparative baseline assessment.

---

### MONTH 2 — Learning How to Learn & Chemistry Fundamentals
* **Primary Focus**: Spaced repetition, memory consolidation, general chemistry foundations (atomic structure, bonding, solutions), academic writing, and research basics.
* **Academic Goal**: Khan Academy Chemistry (Atomic Structure & Periodic Trends); balance 10 complex redox reactions.
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Master precision pattern drafting for standard female blouses and skirts with exact seam allowances.
* **Digital & AI Goal**: Learn advanced Google Scholar search queries and Boolean operators (`AND`, `OR`, `site:edu.ng`).
* **Reading Assignment**: *A Mind for Numbers* by Dr. Barbara Oakley (Focused vs diffuse thinking).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. 1,000-word evidence-based summary on a common Nigerian health challenge (e.g., malaria transmission).
  2. 50 Anki chemistry and biology active recall flashcards tested for 30 consecutive days.

---

### MONTH 3 — The Human Body, Vital Signs & Dexterity
* **Primary Focus**: Human anatomy and physiology overview, cardiovascular system, vital signs interpretation, manual dexterity connection with tailoring, first-aid awareness.
* **Academic Goal**: OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology Chapter 1-4 (Cellular organization, tissues, integumentary, and cardiovascular systems).
* **Healthcare Goal**: Complete Coursera's *Vital Signs: Understanding What the Body Is Telling Us* (Penn).
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Practice intricate hand-stitching (hemming, buttonholes, invisible zips), linking manual precision to surgical/clinical dexterity.
* **Reading Assignment**: *Gifted Hands* by Ben Carson.
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. 10-minute presentation delivered to family/peers on "The Human Heart and Circulation".
  2. Normal Vital Signs Reference Pocket Card.

---

### MONTH 4 — Digital Mastery, Spreadsheets & Financial Costing
* **Primary Focus**: Excel / Google Sheets, data visualization, basic statistics, AI prompt engineering ethics, tailoring costing formulas.
* **Academic Goal**: Khan Academy Healthcare Math & Algebra (metric conversions, drug dosage ratios, IV flow rates).
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Calculate complete unit cost for 3 garments (Fabric + Thread/Zips + Labor Hours + 35% Margin).
* **Digital Goal**: Build a live spreadsheet tracking tailoring revenue, expenses, and personal savings.
* **Reading Assignment**: *Things Fall Apart* by Chinua Achebe (Cultural intelligence & adaptability).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. Functional Excel Budget & Costing Calculator.
  2. 4-Page Digital Tailoring Lookbook designed in Canva.

---

### MONTH 5 — Communication, Public Speaking & Professionalism
* **Primary Focus**: Spoken English clarity, public speaking structure, body language, active listening, professional email etiquette, patient communication.
* **Academic Goal**: Genetics and Inheritance (Duke University on Coursera / Khan Academy).
* **Healthcare Goal**: Study patient-centered communication and empathy in hospital settings.
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Conduct 5 customer intake consultations with structured measurement sheets and delivery commitments.
* **Reading Assignment**: *The Richest Man in Babylon* by George S. Clason (The 10% savings rule).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. Record and review a 5-minute persuasive speech on "Why Primary Healthcare Matters in Edo State".
  2. 3 drafted formal email templates (academic, client, mentorship).

---

### MONTH 6 — Money, Entrepreneurship & First Major Service Project
* **Primary Focus**: Small business economics, pricing without fear, customer retention, ethics in commerce, Mid-Year Community Service Drive.
* **Academic Goal**: Organic Chemistry Basics (Functional groups, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins).
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: "Sewing for Charity" initiative — mend 10 school uniforms or sew reusable cloth tote bags/sanitary supplies for community donation.
* **Volunteering Goal**: Log at least 8 hours of community service with a verified Edo State NGO.
* **Reading Assignment**: *The E-Myth Revisited* by Michael E. Gerber (Systems in business).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. 1-Page Business Model Canvas for a custom tailoring brand in Benin City.
  2. Mid-Year Progress & Volunteer Impact Report.

---

### MONTH 7 — Research Methodology & Critical Thinking
* **Primary Focus**: Formulating research questions, literature synthesis, primary vs secondary evidence, bias detection, research ethics (informed consent).
* **Academic Goal**: Stanford's *Writing in the Sciences* / Coursera Research Methods.
* **Healthcare Goal**: Read and deconstruct 2 open-access PubMed articles on public health in Nigeria.
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Experiment with fabric waste reduction techniques and zero-waste pattern layouts.
* **Reading Assignment**: *The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks* by Rebecca Skloot (Medical ethics).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. 1,500-word beginner research project: "Assessment of Community Awareness on Water Sanitation in Urban Benin City".

---

### MONTH 8 — Healthcare Exploration, Public Health & Ethics
* **Primary Focus**: Comprehensive comparison of healthcare professions (Nursing, Medicine, Pharmacy, MLS, Physiotherapy, Radiography), hospital dynamics, epidemiology.
* **Academic Goal**: Basic Immunology & Antimicrobial Resistance (WHO OpenWHO).
* **Healthcare Goal**: Conduct an informational interview with a licensed nurse or doctor at UBTH or a private hospital.
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Design and construct a pair of professional medical scrubs using durable cotton fabric.
* **Reading Assignment**: *Mountains Beyond Mountains* by Tracy Kidder (Global health & accompaniment).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. Healthcare Career Matrix Reflection (comparing your top 3 career choices).
  2. Custom-tailored medical scrub set with cost breakdown.

---

### MONTH 9 — Emotional Intelligence, Resilience & Leadership
* **Primary Focus**: Emotional regulation, handling academic stress, navigating interpersonal conflict, servant leadership, personal values.
* **Academic Goal**: University Physics Foundations (Mechanics, fluid pressure in blood vessels).
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Train a junior apprentice or peer on a basic sewing technique, demonstrating patience and leadership.
* **Reading Assignment**: *Grit* by Angela Duckworth (Passion & perseverance).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. 14-Day Emotional Trigger & Regulation Log.
  2. SWOT Self-Evaluation & Quarter 3 Soft-Skills Rubric Review.

---

### MONTH 10 — University Simulation Semester
* **Primary Focus**: Full simulation of a university semester load. Independent deadline management, mock lectures, research papers, and self-administered exams.
* **Academic Goal**: Sit timed practice exams covering UNIBEN 100L syllabus: BIO111, CHM111, PHY111, MTH111.
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Manage a full tailoring commission queue while strictly keeping study deadlines.
* **Reading Assignment**: *How to Win Friends and Influence People in the Digital Age* by Dale Carnegie.
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. Mock 100L First Semester Exam Results & Error Analysis.
  2. Time-audit report detailing study vs work balance.

---

### MONTH 11 — Career Portfolio & Professional Launchpad
* **Primary Focus**: Professional CV design, scholarship application readiness, LinkedIn / portfolio setup, interview simulations using STAR method.
* **Academic Goal**: Revise high-yield topics for UNIBEN Post-UTME screening (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English).
* **Healthcare Goal**: Attend a health webinar or university career symposium.
* **Apprenticeship Goal**: Compile a comprehensive physical & digital photography portfolio of all garments created over the past year.
* **Reading Assignment**: *Half of a Yellow Sun* by Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie (History & resilience).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. Professional 2-Page Student CV + Scholarship Application Packet.
  2. Digital Fashion & Skills Portfolio.

---

### MONTH 12 — UNIBEN Readiness & Survival Handbook
* **Primary Focus**: Final university transition, campus geography, accommodation vetting (Hostel vs BDPA/Osasogie), banking, safety, 5.0 CGPA management.
* **Academic Goal**: Complete UNIBEN 100L course outline review and syllabus pre-reading.
* **Reading Assignment**: *Deep Work* by Cal Newport (Undistracted focus).
* **Key Deliverables**:
  1. **"MY UNIVERSITY SURVIVAL & SUCCESS HANDBOOK"** (Comprehensive personal operating manual).
  2. 12-Month Development Portfolio Review & Final Soft-Skills Evaluation.

---

### MONTHS 13 to 18 — University Transition & Advanced Mastery Bridge
* **Month 13**: Intensive UNIBEN Post-UTME CBT screening drills & speed-accuracy optimization.
* **Month 14**: Advanced Medical Terminology & Latin roots for Anatomy and Physiology.
* **Month 15**: Advanced Laboratory Techniques & Biosafety Protocols (Pre-lab preparation).
* **Month 16**: Independent Living Mastery (Bulk meal prepping, domestic budgeting, student banking).
* **Month 17**: Campus Clearance & Administrative Readiness (JAMB CAPS acceptance, document file jackets).
* **Month 18**: Matriculation Launch — First 30 Days execution at UNIBEN Ugbowo Campus.
