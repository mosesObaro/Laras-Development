# Tailoring Apprenticeship & Entrepreneurship Integration Guide
## Professionalizing Craftsmanship, Financial Costing & Small Business Systems

> **Philosophy**:
> Your tailoring apprenticeship is NOT a distraction from your education; it is a **Tier-1 professional asset**.
> It develops fine manual dexterity, 3-dimensional spatial geometry, rigorous patience, client communication, and commercial entrepreneurship.
> A nurse or doctor with tailoring skills has superior hand-eye coordination, suture finesse, and an entrepreneurial mindset that sets her apart for life.

## 2. The Mathematical Pricing & Costing Formula

Never guess what to charge a client. Underpricing leads to financial exhaustion; overpricing without quality leads to lost clients. Use the standard scientific costing formula:

$$\text{Final Price} = \text{Direct Material Cost} + \text{Direct Labor Cost} + \text{Overhead Allocation} + \text{Target Profit Margin}$$

### Step-by-Step Cost Breakdown:
1. **Direct Material Cost ($M$)**:
   * Fabric cost (if provided by you)
   * Threads, zippers, buttons, lining, interfacing (stay/gum-stay), hook & eyes, shoulder pads.
2. **Direct Labor Cost ($L$)**:
   * Calculate your hourly rate (e.g., $\text{₦1,000} - \text{₦1,500}$ per hour).
   * Estimated sewing & cutting hours $\times \text{Hourly Rate}$.
   * *Example*: A detailed gown taking 4 hours = $4 \times \text{₦1,200} = \text{₦4,800}$.
3. **Overhead Allocation ($O$)**:
   * Machine maintenance, electricity/generator fuel for pressing iron, transportation to market, tape measure wear. (Typically $10\% - 15\%$ of labor cost, e.g., $\text{₦600}$).
4. **Target Profit Margin ($P$)**:
   * Add $25\% - 35\%$ margin for business reinvestment and brand growth.

### Complete Example Calculation (Bespoke Female Fitted Gown):
* **Materials (Lining + Stays + Zip + Thread)**: $\text{₦3,200}$
* **Labor (3.5 hours at ₦1,200/hr)**: $\text{₦4,200}$
* **Overhead (Generator power for pressing iron + transport)**: $\text{₦800}$
* **Base Production Cost**: $\text{₦3,200} + \text{₦4,200} + \text{₦800} = \text{₦8,200}$
* **Profit Margin (30% of Base Cost)**: $0.30 \times \text{₦8,200} = \text{₦2,460}$
* **Fair Customer Quotation**: $\mathbf{₦10,660}$ *(or rounded to ₦11,000)*.

## 3. Seven Practical Tailoring Projects

### Project 1: The Measurement & Fitting Precision Project (Month 2)
* Take comprehensive anatomical measurements for 5 different body types.
* Construct an accurate size chart noting bust circumference, waist, high hip, low hip, across-back, shoulder-to-underbust, and full gown length.

### Project 2: Bespoke Everyday Peplum Top (Month 4)
* Draft and cut a lined peplum blouse with invisible zipper insertion and neat armhole bias binding.
* Document exact material cost and sewing duration.

### Project 3: "Sewing for Charity" Community Drive (Month 6)
* Gather durable remnant fabrics or low-cost khaki materials.
* Sew 5 standard primary school uniforms or 10 reusable fabric hygiene bags for a local orphanage or primary school in Benin City.

### Project 4: The 4-Page Digital Lookbook (Month 7)
* Photograph 5 completed bespoke garments on a mannequin or model in natural lighting.
* Use Canva to design a clean, professional digital lookbook featuring garment names, fabrics used, and base prices.

### Project 5: The Medical Scrub Set Prototype (Month 8)
* Research hospital surgical and nursing scrub patterns (V-neck top with patch pockets + drawstring utility trousers).
* Source breathable, durable poly-cotton blend fabric.
* Construct a complete medical scrub set, linking tailoring craftsmanship with healthcare utility.

### Project 6: Customer Service Recovery Simulation (Month 9)
* Draft standard operating procedures (SOPs) for handling common tailoring disputes:
  1. Fabric shortage provided by customer.
  2. Late fabric delivery by customer before an event date.
  3. Fitting adjustments requested after delivery.

### Project 7: The Miniature Tailoring Business Plan (Month 11)
* Create a complete 2-page business plan covering:
  1. Brand Identity & Unique Value Proposition (e.g., *"Precision Medical Scrubs & Modern African Bespoke Fashion"*).
  2. Target Market in Benin City & UNIBEN student community.
  3. Financial forecast & equipment maintenance schedule.
