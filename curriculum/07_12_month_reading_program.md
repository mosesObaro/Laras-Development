## 2. Structured Monthly Reflection Framework

For every book, complete the **4-Part Reading Journal Entry**:

1. **Core Thesis (3–4 sentences)**: What is the single most important argument or lesson the author is making?
2. **Key Concepts (3 bullet points)**: Which three specific frameworks, ideas, or quotes resonated with you?
3. **Personal Connection (1 paragraph)**: How does this book directly apply to your tailoring apprenticeship, your science studies, or your preparation for UNIBEN?
4. **Actionable Commitments (2 specific actions)**: What two concrete behaviors will you start, stop, or modify in your life based on this reading?

## 3. Writing Exercise Prompts

* **Month 3 Prompt (*Gifted Hands*)**:
  > *"Analyze how physical craft skills (such as tailoring, sketching, or model assembly) contribute to neurosurgical and clinical precision. How do you plan to leverage your tailoring experience during clinical nursing rotations at UBTH?"*
* **Month 7 Prompt (*The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks*)**:
  > *"Define informed consent in healthcare. Write a 1,000-word essay explaining why institutional review boards and patient autonomy are vital safeguards in modern medical research in Nigeria."*
* **Month 12 Prompt (*Deep Work*)**:
  > *"Design your personal 'Deep Work Protocol' for UNIBEN 100-Level. Detail where, when, and how you will execute 90 minutes of undistracted study daily while living on campus."*
