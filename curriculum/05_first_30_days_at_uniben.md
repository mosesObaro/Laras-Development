## 2. Step-by-Step Breakdown

### Phase 1: Admission & Financial Clearance (Days 1 – 14)
1. **JAMB CAPS Acceptance**:
   * Log in to the official JAMB Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS).
   * Confirm and click **ACCEPT ADMISSION**. Print original JAMB Admission Letter in color.
2. **UNIBEN Acceptance Fee Payment**:
   * Log into your UNIBEN WAeUP Kofa applicant portal (`https://waeup.uniben.edu`).
   * Generate Remita Retrieval Reference (RRR) for the Acceptance Fee.
   * Pay online via debit card or at any commercial bank on campus (UBA, First Bank, EcoBank along Main Gate).
   * Print 5 copies of the official payment receipt.
3. **Required Documents to Assemble**:
   * 15 red-background recent passport photographs.
   * Original & 5 photocopies of WAEC / NECO Statement of Result.
   * Original & 5 photocopies of JAMB Result Slip & JAMB Admission Letter.
   * Birth Certificate or National Population Commission Declaration of Age.
   * Local Government of Origin Identification Letter.
   * Two letters of attestation (from a clergyman, lawyer, civil servant, or school principal).
   * Physical clearance file jackets (obtained on campus).

---

### Phase 2: Physical Clearance & Course Registration (Days 15 – 21)
1. **Physical Clearance**:
   * Report early (by 7:30 AM) to the **Main Auditorium** or your Faculty Clearance Venue.
   * Submit your clearance file for verification by faculty clearance officers.
   * Receive your official UNIBEN Clearance Slip and Matriculation Number.
2. **Course Registration (100L First Semester)**:
   * Log into the WAeUP portal and select all compulsory 100-Level pre-med/science courses (`BIO111`, `BIO112`, `CHM111`, `CHM112`, `PHY111`, `PHY112`, `MTH111`, `GST111`, `GST112`).
   * Print 4 copies of your Course Registration Form.
   * Take the printed forms to your **Departmental Course Adviser** for review and official physical signature.
   * Submit signed copies to the Departmental Office, Faculty Office, and Exams & Records, retaining one original signed copy in your personal file.

---

### Phase 3: Medical Clearance & Campus Settling (Days 22 – 30)
1. **Health Centre Medical Screening**:
   * Visit the **UNIBEN Health Centre** (near Hall 1 / Hall 2).
   * Complete routine blood screening (Packed Cell Volume / PCV, Genotype, Blood Group) and submit your chest X-ray report.
   * Obtain your validated Medical Clearance Certificate.
2. **John Harris Library (JHL) Registration**:
   * Take two passport photographs and your student clearance slip to the JHL circulation desk.
   * Collect your library borrower's card and attend the brief 30-minute electronic catalog orientation.

## 3. Accommodation & Living Options in Benin City

| Option | Pros | Cons | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **On-Campus Hostels** (Hall 1 & Hall 2 for Females) | Highest security, shortest walk to lecture halls and health centre, 24/7 student environment. | Shared room density (multiple roommates), competitive allocation. | **Highly Recommended for Year 1** |
| **BDPA Estate (Off-Campus)** | Quiet residential layout, standard flats, mature environment, safe main streets. | Requires 10-15 minute walk or shuttle to Main Gate, higher rental cost. | **Top Off-Campus Choice** |
| **Osasogie (Near UBTH Gate)** | Immediate walking access to teaching hospital and medical classrooms. | High demand from medical/nursing students, premium rents. | **Ideal for 200L-500L Clinical Years** |
| **Ekosodin (Behind Back Gate)** | Lower rents, high youth vitality, close to faculty buildings. | High crowd density, noisy, requires strict curfew adherence. | **Caution: Stick to verified compounds only** |

## 4. Academic Survival Principles for Week 1 of Lectures

1. **The 8:00 AM Rule**: Pre-med lecture halls (e.g., 1000-Seater Lecture Theatre, Law Auditorium, or Basement) fill up 30 minutes before 8:00 AM. Arrive by 7:30 AM to secure a front seat for clear hearing and audio recording.
2. **Form a Focused Study Triad**: Connect with 2 serious, disciplined classmates within your first week. Study together for 90 minutes every evening at John Harris Library.
3. **Course Outline Mastery**: Request the official syllabus from each lecturer during the first lecture. Cross-reference topics with your pre-university notes and Anki flashcards.
4. **Guard Your Health**: Drink clean bottled or sachet water from vetted campus vendors; never skip breakfast before early laboratory practicals.
