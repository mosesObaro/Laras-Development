# Quarterly Soft-Skills Assessment Rubric (1–5 Scale)
## Evidence-Based Evaluation Across 15 Core Development Competencies

> **Assessment Rule**:
> Course completion alone does NOT justify a score of 4 or 5.
> Scores must be backed by documented real-world **EVIDENCE** (e.g., recorded presentations, completed garments, client reviews, logged volunteer hours, budget ledgers).

---

## The 5-Point Scoring Scale Defined

* **Level 1 (Novice / Struggling)**: Displays significant anxiety, inconsistency, or misunderstanding; avoids tasks or relies entirely on close supervision.
* **Level 2 (Developing / Inconsistent)**: Understands theory but executes inconsistently; struggles under time pressure or unexpected difficulty.
* **Level 3 (Competent / Baseline)**: Capable of independent execution under normal conditions; demonstrates reliable baseline competence.
* **Level 4 (Proficient / High Performance)**: Executes smoothly, adapts gracefully to unexpected challenges, and demonstrates proactive initiative.
* **Level 5 (Mastery / Role Model)**: Effortless poise, inspires peers, teaches others, and maintains exceptional standards under high stress.

## Detailed Competency Rubrics

| Competency | Level 1 Description | Level 3 (Baseline) | Level 5 (Mastery) | Required Evidence for Level 4/5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **2. Public Speaking** | Severe stage fright, reads verbatim from slides without eye contact. | Delivers 5-minute talk with clear slides and audible voice. | Captivates audience for 15 mins, tells stories, handles Q&A calmly. | Video recording of 10-min presentation on human biology. |
| **3. | **4. Problem Solving** | Panics during disruptions; treats superficial symptoms emotionally. | Breaks problems into root causes (5 Whys) and implements fixes. | Anticipates bottlenecks, designs robust contingency protocols. | Documented resolution of a tailoring or study scheduling bottleneck. |
| **5. Emotional Intelligence** | Highly defensive, volatile under stress, lacks empathy for others. | Recognizes personal emotional triggers, listens to constructive critique. | Exceptional calm under pressure, genuine empathy, regulates emotions. | 14-day emotional regulation log + reflection notes. |
| **6. Confidence** | Paralyzed by impostor syndrome, avoids challenging assignments. | Comfortable with strengths, accepts mistakes as learning data. | Quiet self-assurance without arrogance; embraces high-stakes challenges. | Completion of challenging public project or leadership task. |
| **7. Leadership** | Passes blame to others, shies away from team responsibility. | Takes ownership of assigned deliverables, coordinates peers fairly. | Servant leader: empowers teammates, takes full responsibility for outcomes. | Coordination of a community service or study group project. |
| **8. Teamwork** | Prefers solitary work to avoid conflict, struggles to compromise. | Collaborates cooperatively, respects diverse team roles. | Mediates team conflicts, elevates peers, builds psychological safety. | Peer or mentor evaluation from collaborative volunteering. |

Financial Discipline** | Spends impulsively, zero savings, vulnerable to get-rich-quick scams. | Budgets monthly income, saves $\ge 10\%$, understands inflation. | Rigorous financial ledger, plans emergency fund, spots fraud instantly. | 3-month verified personal budget and savings ledger. |
| **11. Professionalism** | Frequently late, sloppy presentation, blurs boundaries. | Punctual, neat, adheres to workplace and academic etiquette. | Exemplifies integrity, confidentiality, and flawless punctuality. | 30 consecutive days of 10-minute early arrivals logged. |

| **15. Learning Ability** | Passive memorization (cramming), repeats ineffective study habits. | Uses active recall, spaced repetition, and concept summaries. | Rapidly synthesizes complex concepts using the Feynman Technique. | 500 Anki flashcards created and maintained for 60+ days. |
