# Healthcare Foundations & Clinical Safety Framework

> [!CAUTION]
> **CRITICAL BOUNDARY & SAFETY PRINCIPLE FOR PRE-UNIVERSITY STUDENTS**:
> A pre-university student is NOT a licensed healthcare practitioner.
> Under NO circumstances should an unqualified teenager perform invasive procedures (e.g., administering injections, setting IV lines, making clinical diagnoses, prescribing medications, or suturing wounds).
> All pre-university healthcare exposure must be strictly non-clinical: health education, hygiene advocacy, observation, patient communication, empathy, first-aid awareness, and public health data collection under qualified professional supervision.

## 2. Medical Terminology Decoded

Medical terminology is built like a puzzle from three primary building blocks:

### A. Common Prefixes (Beginnings)
* **Brady-**: Abnormally slow (e.g., *Bradycardia* = slow heart rate $< 60\text{ bpm}$)
* **Tachy-**: Abnormally fast (e.g., *Tachycardia* = rapid heart rate $> 100\text{ bpm}$)
* **Hyper-**: High / excessive (e.g., *Hypertension* = high blood pressure)
* **Hypo-**: Low / deficient (e.g., *Hypoglycemia* = low blood sugar)
* **Dys-**: Difficult / painful (e.g., *Dyspnea* = shortness of breath; *Dysuria* = painful urination)
* **Poly-**: Many / excessive (e.g., *Polyuria* = excessive urination; *Polydipsia* = excessive thirst)
* **Hemi-**: Half (e.g., *Hemiplegia* = paralysis of one side of the body)
* **A- / An-**: Without / lack of (e.g., *Anemia* = deficiency of red blood cells; *Apnea* = cessation of breathing)

### B. Common Root Words (Core Anatomy)
* **Cardi / Cardia**: Heart
* **Pulmon / Pneumo**: Lungs / Air
* **Gastr / Gastro**: Stomach
* **Hepat / Hepato**: Liver
* **Nephr / Reno**: Kidney
* **Encephal / Cerebro**: Brain
* **Hema / Hemato / Emia**: Blood
* **Derm / Dermato**: Skin
* **Osteo**: Bone
* **Myo**: Muscle
* **Neur / Neuro**: Nerve / Nervous system

### C. Common Suffixes (Endings)
* **-itis**: Inflammation (e.g., *Appendicitis*, *Hepatitis*, *Gastritis*)
* **-ectomy**: Surgical removal (e.g., *Appendectomy*, *Mastectomy*)
* **-ostomy**: Creating an artificial opening (e.g., *Colostomy*)
* **-otomy**: Cutting into an organ (e.g., *Laparotomy*)
* **-pathy**: Disease / disorder (e.g., *Cardiomyopathy*, *Neuropathy*)
* **-scopy**: Visual examination with an instrument (e.g., *Endoscopy*, *Colonoscopy*)
* **-megaly**: Enlargement (e.g., *Hepatomegaly*, *Splenomegaly*)
* **-penia**: Deficiency (e.g., *Leukopenia* = low white blood cell count)
* **-emia**: Blood condition (e.g., *Bacteremia*, *Uremia*)

## 3. Four Pillars of Healthcare Ethics

1. **Autonomy**: Respecting the patient's right to make informed decisions about their own body and care without coercion (Informed Consent).
2. **Beneficence**: Acting in the best interest of the patient; providing care that promotes health and well-being.
3. **Non-Maleficence**: *"Primum non nocere"* (First, do no harm). Ensuring treatments do not inflict unnecessary injury or risk.
4. **Justice**: Treating all patients fairly and equitably without discrimination based on tribe, religion, gender, or wealth.

## 4. Vital Signs: Normal Adult Baseline Values

| Vital Sign | Normal Range (Resting Adult) | Clinical Terms for Deviations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Heart Rate (Pulse)** | 60 – 100 beats per minute (bpm) | $<60$: Bradycardia \| $>100$: Tachycardia |
| **Blood Pressure (BP)** | Systolic: 90 – 120 mmHg<br>Diastolic: 60 – 80 mmHg | $>130/80$: Hypertension \| $<90/60$: Hypotension |
| **Respiratory Rate (RR)** | 12 – 20 breaths per minute | $<12$: Bradypnea \| $>20$: Tachypnea |
| **Body Temperature** | 36.5°C – 37.5°C (97.7°F – 99.5°F) | $>37.5°C$: Pyrexia / Fever \| $<35.0°C$: Hypothermia |
| **Oxygen Saturation ($\text{SpO}_2$)** | 95% – 100% on room air | $<95$: Hypoxia |

## 5. WHO 6-Step Hand Hygiene Technique (Clean Hands Save Lives)

1. **Step 1**: Palm to palm with soap and running water.
2. **Step 2**: Right palm over left dorsum with interlaced fingers and vice versa.
3. **Step 3**: Palm to palm with fingers interlaced.
4. **Step 4**: Backs of fingers to opposing palms with fingers interlocked.
5. **Step 5**: Rotational rubbing of left thumb clasped in right palm and vice versa.
6. **Step 6**: Rotational rubbing backwards and forwards with clasped fingertips in palm.
* **Duration**: At least 40–60 seconds for soap and water; 20–30 seconds for alcohol hand rub.
