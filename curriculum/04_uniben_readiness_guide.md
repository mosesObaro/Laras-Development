## 1. Relevant Healthcare Faculties & Academic Divisions

At the University of Benin, healthcare and biological programs are housed primarily within the **College of Medical Sciences**, the **Faculty of Pharmacy**, and the **Faculty of Life Sciences** at the **Ugbowo Campus**:

### A. College of Medical Sciences
1. **School of Basic Medical Sciences (SBMS)**:
   * **Department of Nursing Science**: Offers 5-year Bachelor of Nursing Science (B.N.Sc) with dual professional registration (RN, RM, RPHN) accredited by NMCN.
   * **Department of Medical Laboratory Science (B.MLS)**: 5-year diagnostic science program accredited by MLSCN.
   * **Department of Physiotherapy (B.PT / DPT)**: 5-year medical rehabilitation program accredited by MRTB.
   * **Department of Radiography & Radiation Science (B.Rad)**: 5-year imaging program accredited by RRBN.
   * **Department of Anatomy (B.Sc)**: 4-year fundamental anatomical research and histology program.
   * **Department of Physiology (B.Sc)**: 4-year human cellular and systemic physiology program.
   * **Department of Medical Biochemistry (B.Sc)**: 4-year clinical biochemistry program.

2. **School of Medicine**:
   * Offers 6-year **MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery)** accredited by MDCN. Clinical training conducted at the **University of Benin Teaching Hospital (UBTH)**.

3. **School of Dentistry**:
   * Offers 6-year **BDS (Bachelor of Dental Surgery)** accredited by MDCN.

### B. Faculty of Pharmacy
* Offers 6-year **Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D)** accredited by PCN. UNIBEN is renowned as the pioneer of the Pharm.D program in Nigeria.

### C. Faculty of Life Sciences
* Houses Departments of Biochemistry, Microbiology, Animal & Environmental Biology (AEB), Plant Biology & Biotechnology (PBB), and Environmental Management.

## 2. Admission Requirements & Subject Combinations

### O'Level Requirements (WAEC / NECO / NABTEB):
* Minimum of **5 Credit Passes** in:
  1. English Language
  2. Mathematics
  3. Biology
  4. Chemistry
  5. Physics
* Obtained in **not more than TWO sittings** (Single sitting strongly preferred for Medicine and Dentistry).

### UTME (JAMB) Subject Combination:
* **Compulsory 4 Subjects**:
  1. English Language
  2. Biology
  3. Chemistry
  4. Physics

### Post-UTME Screening:
* UNIBEN conducts a Computer-Based Test (CBT) covering the 4 UTME subjects.
* Aggregate Score Formula:
  $$\text{Aggregate} = \left(\frac{\text{JAMB Score}}{8}\right) + \left(\frac{\text{Post-UTME Score (out of 100)}}{2}\right)$$
  *(Target an aggregate of $\ge 70\%$ for high-demand programs like Nursing, Medicine, and Pharmacy).*

## 3. UNIBEN Academic Structure & 5.0 CGPA System

### Course Credit System:
* Each course is assigned **Credit Units** (typically 1 to 4 units) reflecting weekly lecture and practical hours.
* Example: `BIO111` (3 units) carries 3 times the GPA weight of `BIO112` (1 unit practical).

### UNIBEN Grading Scale:

| Score Range (%) | Letter Grade | Grade Point (GP) | Remark |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **70 – 100** | **A** | **5.0** | Excellent |
| **60 – 69** | **B** | **4.0** | Very Good |
| **50 – 59** | **C** | **3.0** | Good |
| **45 – 49** | **D** | **2.0** | Fair / Average |
| **40 – 44** | **E** | **1.0** | Pass |
| **0 – 39** | **F** | **0.0** | Fail (Must repeat course) |

### CGPA Degree Classifications:
* **4.50 – 5.00**: **First Class Honours**
* **3.50 – 4.49**: **Second Class Honours (Upper Division / 2:1)**
* **2.40 – 3.49**: **Second Class Honours (Lower Division / 2:2)**
* **1.50 – 2.39**: **Third Class Honours**
* **1.00 – 1.49**: **Pass**
* **Below 1.00**: **Academic Probation / Withdrawal**

### How to Calculate CGPA:
$$\text{GPA} = \frac{\sum (\text{Course Units} \times \text{Grade Point})}{\sum (\text{Total Course Units})}$$

*Example*:
* `BIO111` (3 units, Grade A = 5 GP) $\rightarrow 3 \times 5 = 15$
* `CHM111` (3 units, Grade A = 5 GP) $\rightarrow 3 \times 5 = 15$
* `PHY111` (3 units, Grade B = 4 GP) $\rightarrow 3 \times 4 = 12$
* `MTH111` (3 units, Grade B = 4 GP) $\rightarrow 3 \times 4 = 12$
* `GST111` (2 units, Grade A = 5 GP) $\rightarrow 2 \times 5 = 10$
* **Total Points** = $15 + 15 + 12 + 12 + 10 = 64$
* **Total Units** = $3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 2 = 14$
* **First Semester GPA** = $\frac{64}{14} = \mathbf{4.57}$ *(First Class Honours standing)*.

## 5. Campus Geography & Safety Principles

### Core Ugbowo Campus Hubs:
* **Main Gate**: Along Benin-Lagos Expressway (Access to shuttle buses, commercial banks, and Kofa administrative portal).
* **Main Auditorium & Sports Complex**: Venue for physical matriculation clearance and university ceremonies.
* **John Harris Library (JHL)**: The central academic library with physical study carrels and e-library databases.
* **UNIBEN Health Centre**: Located near Hall 1, providing primary healthcare and medical clearance.
* **UBTH Gate / Osasogie**: Connects the university directly to the teaching hospital complex (vital for clinical nursing, medicine, and pharmacy students).

### Key Rules of Student Safety:
1. Never walk alone through unlit, isolated pathways (e.g., Capitol buffer zones) after 8:00 PM; use designated campus shuttles or walk in vetted peer groups.
2. Store emergency contacts for UNIBEN Chief Security Officer (CSO) and hall wardens on speed dial.
3. Guard academic logins (WAeUP Kofa password and JAMB CAPS credentials) strictly against third-party cyber cafes.
