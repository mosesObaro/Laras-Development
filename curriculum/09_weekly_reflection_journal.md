# Weekly & Daily Reflection Framework
## The 15-Minute Daily Habit of Metacognition and Growth

> "We do not learn from experience... we learn from reflecting on experience."
> — John Dewey

---

## 1. Daily 5-Minute Evening Reflection Prompt

Every evening before bed, write brief responses to these 3 prompts:

1. **Win of the Day**: What is one thing I executed with excellence or discipline today (in tailoring, study, reading, or character)?
2. **Friction / Lesson**: What friction or distraction challenged me today, and what will I do differently tomorrow?
3. **Gratitude & Anchor**: What am I genuinely grateful for today, and what is my primary priority for tomorrow morning?

---
