### High-Yield Healthcare Applications:
* **Osmosis and Tonicity**: Understanding why intravenous (IV) fluids must be isotonic (0.9% Normal Saline) to prevent erythrocyte hemolysis (hypotonic) or crenation (hypertonic).
* **Sickle Cell Disease (HbSS)**: Point mutation in beta-globin chain (glutamic acid replaced by valine at position 6), leading to red blood cell polymerization under hypoxic stress, vaso-occlusive crises, and anemia in Nigeria.
* **Cardiac Conduction**: SA node (natural pacemaker) $\rightarrow$ AV node $\rightarrow$ Bundle of His $\rightarrow$ Purkinje fibers; translating electrical impulses into mechanical ventricular contraction.
* **Renal Filtration**: Glomerular filtration, tubular reabsorption, and secretion in the nephron; mechanism of action of anti-diuretic hormone (ADH) and aldosterone.

## 2. Chemistry (Inorganic, Organic & General)

### Core Modules:
1. **Atomic Structure & Chemical Bonding**:
   * Quantum numbers, electronic configurations ($s, p, d, f$ orbitals).
   * Ionic, covalent, coordinate (dative), and hydrogen bonding (crucial for DNA base pairing and protein folding).
2. **Solutions, Concentrations & Stoichiometry**:
   * Molarity ($M = \text{moles} / \text{liters}$), Molality, Normality, and percentage concentrations ($w/v, v/v$).
   * Standard solutions and dilution calculations ($C_1V_1 = C_2V_2$).
3. **Acids, Bases, Salts & Buffer Systems**:
   * Arrhenius, Bronsted-Lowry, and Lewis definitions of acids and bases.
   * pH scale: $\text{pH} = -\log_{10}[\text{H}^+]$.
   * Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation for biological buffers:
     $$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log\left(\frac{[\text{A}^-]}{[\text{HA}]}\right)$$
   * The bicarbonate buffer system maintaining human arterial blood pH strictly between $7.35$ and $7.45$:
     $$\text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightleftharpoons \text{H}_2\text{CO}_3 \rightleftharpoons \text{H}^+ + \text{HCO}_3^-$$
4. **Organic Chemistry & Functional Groups**:
   * Hydrocarbons, alkyl halides, alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters, amines, and amides.
   * Isomerism: Structural, geometric ($cis/trans$), and optical isomerism (chirality in drug molecules like Thalidomide).
   * Saponification and lipid biochemistry.

## 3. Physics (Mechanics, Waves, Optics & Electricity)

### Core Modules:
1. **Mechanics & Fluid Statics**:
   * Newton's laws of motion, work, energy, power, and momentum.
   * Fluid pressure: $P = \rho g h$.
   * Poiseuille's Law of vascular blood flow:
     $$Q = \frac{\pi \Delta P r^4}{8 \eta L}$$
     *(Note how reducing blood vessel radius $r$ by half decreases flow 16-fold, explaining hypertension risks).*
2. **Waves, Sound & Optics**:
   * Transverse vs longitudinal waves, frequency, wavelength, speed ($v = f \lambda$).
   * Doppler Effect (used in Doppler ultrasound to measure fetal heartbeat and vascular blood flow velocity).
   * Geometrical optics: Snell's Law of refraction, lens equations, optical instruments (microscopes, ophthalmoscopes).
   * Visual defects and corrections: Myopia (diverging/concave lens), Hyperopia (converging/convex lens).
3. **Electricity, Magnetism & Radiation**:
   * Ohm's Law ($V = IR$), electric potential, capacitance in cell membranes.
   * Electromagnetic spectrum: X-rays, gamma rays, ultraviolet, visible light, infrared.
   * Medical imaging physics: X-ray attenuation, ultrasound acoustic impedance, and MRI nuclear magnetic resonance.

## 4. Mathematics & Healthcare Calculations

### Core Modules:
1. **Algebra & Proportions**:
   * Linear and quadratic equations, system of linear equations.
   * Direct and inverse proportions.
2. **Clinical Dosage & Drip Rate Calculations**:
   * Basic Formula:
     $$\text{Dose to Administer} = \frac{\text{Desired Dose}}{\text{Dose on Hand}} \times \text{Quantity on Hand}$$
   * IV Drip Rate Formula:
     $$\text{Flow Rate (drops/min)} = \frac{\text{Total Volume (mL)} \times \text{Drop Factor (drops/mL)}}{\text{Time (minutes)}}$$
3. **Probability, Statistics & Scientific Graphing**:
   * Measures of central tendency: Mean, Median, Mode.
   * Measures of dispersion: Variance, Standard Deviation, Interquartile Range.
   * Understanding normal distributions, $p$-values ($p < 0.05$ significance threshold), and confidence intervals.
